Wafa Hospital System v4.3.16
============================

Final access-control and Ministry portal update built on v4.3.15.

1) Dedicated Social Service account
-----------------------------------
Username: social.service
Password: Social@2026
Role: social_worker

- Logs directly into the inpatient Social & Psychological Service screen.
- Does not receive access to inpatient administration, rehabilitation, finance, MOH portal,
  patients, billing, reports, or system administration unless additional permissions are
  explicitly assigned later by an authorized administrator.
- Can open inpatient cases by national ID, save the social/psychological assessment, and
  print/export its reports according to the social-service permission set.

2) Users & Permissions by hospital department/branch
-----------------------------------------------------
- Added role templates for Reception, Rehabilitation Specialist, Social Worker,
  Inpatient Finance, Ministry of Health and Custom accounts.
- Added quick department/branch permission cards for:
  Reception & patients, clinics & doctors, inpatient administration, inpatient rehab,
  inpatient social service, inpatient finance, Ministry portal, lab/diagnostics,
  accounting/billing, reports, and system administration.
- Detailed View/Create/Update/Delete/Print/Export/Fee permissions remain available below
  the quick selector.
- The protected treasury owner (Eng.Mohammed_Moqbil) is the only account allowed to grant
  financial permissions. Once granted to a custom branch account, those exact financial
  permissions work in the related pages/API routes.
- The protected treasury account itself remains protected from modification by other users.

3) Ministry of Health - Add hospital patient
--------------------------------------------
- MOH users with moh_portal.create can add a new hospital patient directly from the MOH portal.
- National ID lookup uses the Civil Registry integration and previews name, gender, birth date,
  address and ID before creation.
- The created patient is linked to Ministry of Health coverage and receives a hospital medical
  serial number.
- Existing-patient detection prevents accidental duplicate creation.

Database notes
--------------
No new migration is required for v4.3.16.
Run `php artisan db:seed` after upgrading so the new social-service role/user and the expanded
permission templates are synchronized. The login bootstrap also repairs the built-in Social
Service and MOH accounts if an older database is used.
