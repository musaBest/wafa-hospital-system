Wafaa Hospital System v4.3.38

- Added a new inpatient branch: Internal Physical Therapy.
- Renamed visible Arabic wording from التنويم to المبيت where applicable.
- All active inpatient admissions appear automatically in the new internal PT branch without re-entering patient demographics or diagnosis.
- Added therapist/nurse management with male/female scope:
  * Male staff show male inpatient cases.
  * Female staff show women and children cases.
- Added checkbox assignment of patients to each therapist/nurse.
- Added configurable session counts and assignment notes.
- Added patient evaluation modal. Saved evaluations are sent to the inpatient/rehab report history for the patient.
- Added PT statistics reports with filters by date, gender, ward, age range, children-only and search by name/ID/medical serial.
- Added report columns selector and custom extra report fields.
- Added PDF, Word and Excel export for internal PT reports.
- Added therapist assignment summary table.
- Added inpatient_pt permissions to the system permission catalog and user-management screen.
