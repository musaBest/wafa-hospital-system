WAFA Hospital System v4.3.70

Hotfix: registration/payment/doctor queue data persistence and linking.

Changes:
- Doctor queue now receives the paid case immediately after payment confirmation and remains visible after refresh.
- The backend repairs/creates missing visit and queue rows from the central cashier workflow if an older browser/local cache interrupted the sync.
- Registration desk has a persistent "آخر الزيارات المسجلة" list loaded from the backend database, so newly registered visits do not disappear after refreshing the page.
- Registration workflow is bridged to the central cashier workflow for any visits.create user, not only a locally cached cashier session.
- Doctor portal accepts workflow statuses: paid, collected, audited.

Run after extracting:
cd wafa-api
php artisan migrate --force
php artisan optimize:clear
