Wafaa Hospital System v4.3.49
=================================

Outpatient Physical Therapy
- Removed coverage class, employee name, relationship, change specialist, and decision link/number from case registration.
- Session fee is optional.
- Added "coverage entity covers full cost"; when enabled, the session fee is stored as zero and reports show the covering entity.
- Case registration now has one clear Save Case button instead of record navigation/action toolbar.
- Added New Patient action and row-level Delete from PT Department action with confirmation; deleting a PT case also removes its PT sessions, not the hospital's master patient record.
- Patient diagnosis remains visible in the patient/case tables.
- Report quick actions are now only:
  1) All Sessions Count (popup)
  2) Daily Sessions Count (popup with date selector)
- PDF / Word / Excel export remain available.
- Word export uses collapsed, explicit full cell borders to avoid missing table lines.

Inpatient reports

Database
- Added outpatient_pt_cases.coverage_covers_cost.
- Added pt_head role permissions and dedicated account.
